How the Health Score is Calculated

OptiForge computes your Health Score using a deterministic, documented penalty formula based on verified diagnostics:

Health Score = 100 - (High × 12 + Medium × 6 + Low × 2)
Minimum score floor: 10 / 100
Severity Weightings:
  • High Impact (-12 pts): Memory leaks, hot loops, parser-blocking assets, and heavy dead libraries.
  • Medium Impact (-6 pts): Uncompressed images, identical duplicate files, slow TTFB, layout shifts (CLS).
  • Low Impact (-2 pts): Unminified scripts, minor unused CSS rules, small asset redundancies.
